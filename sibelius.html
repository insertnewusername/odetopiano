Overview

Jean Silelius is one of Finland's most celebrated composers. He once was originally studying law, before turning to the world of music, through songs like Finlandia, he gained a lifetime pension from the government, and was very famous in Finland and around the world

Famous Compositions

Noteworthy Information

  • Sibelius had synesthesia, he literally saw musical chords as colours
  • When Russia was censoring and oppressing Finland stuff, Sibelius used his music as a kind of protest hidden under names that bypassed the stuff
  • The Finnish senate had a vote and ended up giving Sibelius an annual lifetime pension
