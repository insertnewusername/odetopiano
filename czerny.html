Overview

Czerny was one of the most influential piano teachers in Western music history, performing Mozart's Concertos at 9, at the age of 10, he was taught by Beethoven, who was so impressed that he teached Czerny free of charge, later memorising all of Beethoven's solo piano works. He wrote hundreds of piano exercises and etudes, such as school of velocity
